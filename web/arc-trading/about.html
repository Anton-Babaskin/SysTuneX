Who we are

The company

Arc Trading (Arc Trading İç ve Dış Ticaret Limited Şirketi) is a trusted metal trader and strategic partner of metallurgical enterprises worldwide. Specializing in high-quality ferrous and non-ferrous metals, we serve industries across the CIS, Europe, and beyond.

Our extensive global network ensures reliable, on-time deliveries. Committed to innovation and transparency, we offer customized, scalable solutions to drive business growth. Whether in metals or chemicals, Arc Trading empowers industries to succeed in a competitive market.

The office is in Istanbul, at the crossing point between the producing mills of the CIS and the buyers of Europe, the Middle East and North Africa. That position is what lets us move from an enquiry to a firm offer quickly, and to hold a shipment together when a route has to change.

What we trade

  • Long products — rebar, wire rod, plate, channels, beams, angles, rods, mine stands
  • Semi-finished — pig iron, slabs, square billets
  • Ferroalloys — ferrosilicon, ferrosilicon manganese
  • Chemicals — coke products, ammonium sulfate, coal tar, crude coal benzene
  • Raw materials and slag — iron ore fines, limestone, granulated and converter slag, quicklime
  • Industrial gases — argon, oxygen, nitrogen, krypton-xenon and neon-helium mixtures

Process

How we work

01

Enquiry

You send the position, the grade or standard, the volume and the destination. Anything you already know narrows the offer.

02

Offer

We come back with availability from the mills in the network, price, lead time and the delivery terms that fit the route.

03

Contract

Specification, tolerances, inspection, packing, payment and shipping terms are fixed in writing before anything is booked.

04

Production and inspection

The lot is produced to the agreed specification and released against the mill certificate; third-party inspection on request.

05

Shipment

Sea, rail or road, or a combination of them. Documents follow the shipment so customs clearance is not waiting on us.

06

Delivery

Handover on the agreed terms, with one point of contact from the first enquiry through to the last document.
