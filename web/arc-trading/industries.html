How it works in practice

Tell us the application, not just the position

A rebar order for a bridge deck and a rebar order for a warehouse slab are not the same order. The grade, the diameter spread, the bar length, the tolerance the inspector will check and the packing that survives the site all change with the job.

So the more you say about where the material is going, the closer the first offer lands. If you already work to a drawing or a standard, send it — we will quote against it rather than against a generic description.
