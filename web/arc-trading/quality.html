Quality and standards

All products comply with GOST and international standards of technical and physical indicators. What that means for a shipment is set out below.

Before loading

What is fixed, and when

01

Specification

Grade, standard, dimensions, tolerances and surface condition are written into the contract before production is booked. Nothing is left to be interpreted at the mill.

02

Mill certificate

Each lot is released against the producing mill’s certificate of quality: heat number, chemical composition and mechanical properties for that heat.

03

Independent inspection

Third-party inspection at loading where the contract calls for it, or where you ask for it. The report travels with the shipping documents.

04

Marking and packing

Bundling, tagging and marking to the agreed spec, so the lot can be identified against its certificate on arrival.

05

Claims

If something arrives outside specification, it is handled against the certificate and the inspection report rather than against opinion. Tell us early and keep the marking intact.

Standards

GOST, and whatever else the contract names

Most of the tonnage in the partner network is produced to GOST. Where a buyer works to EN, ASTM, DIN or a national equivalent, the grade is matched and the equivalence is written into the specification — not assumed.

Common equivalences

A starting point, not a substitute for the contract

Grades that look alike across systems rarely match on every indicator. The table below is how conversations usually start; what actually ships is agreed against the full specification.

PositionUsually specified by
RebarGrade and class, bar diameter, length, rib pattern, GOST or EN reference
Wire rodSteel grade, coil diameter and weight, carbon range, scale condition
HRS plateGrade, thickness, width, length, flatness and edge condition
Beams and channelsProfile number, grade, length, web and flange tolerance
Slabs and billetsGrade, section, length, casting route, internal quality requirement
FerroalloysElement content, size fraction, packing
Coke productsAsh, sulphur, moisture, size, mechanical strength
Send the standard you work to with the enquiry. Matching it at the offer stage is ordinary work; matching it after production is not.
